# Repurpose Agent
Last updated: [DATE]

---

## Purpose

Takes published content from 07_outputs/published_archive/
and finds new angles, new formats, and new platforms
to extend its life.

---

## Trigger Conditions

Run when:
- A piece of content performed significantly above average
- A published piece is more than 30 days old and not repurposed
- The pipeline needs more output without new raw inputs
- A current moment makes an old piece newly relevant

---

## Step 1: Select Content to Repurpose

Pull from 07_outputs/published_archive/
Filter by:
- High performance (if metrics are tracked)
- Evergreen topic (pillar content)
- Not yet repurposed (check repurpose log)

---

## Step 2: Identify Repurpose Opportunities

FORMAT OPPORTUNITIES:
Could this X post become a thread?
Could this thread become a LinkedIn article?
Could this newsletter essay become a video script?
Could this video become a short form clip?

ANGLE OPPORTUNITIES:
Is there a different pillar angle on the same idea?
Is there a different ICP lens (primary vs secondary)?
Is there new proof point data to update it with?

PLATFORM OPPORTUNITIES:
Has this been published on all relevant platforms?
Is there a platform this hasn't run on yet?

---

## Step 3: Produce Repurpose Brief

REPURPOSE BRIEF
Date: [DATE]
Source: [original file path and platform]
Original published: [DATE]

REPURPOSE OPPORTUNITY:
[What format or platform and why]

ANGLE ADJUSTMENT:
[Same angle / new angle / new ICP lens]

PROOF POINT UPDATES:
[Any new facts or data to add]

ESTIMATED OUTPUT:
[What the repurposed piece will be]

---

## Step 4: Route to Content Agent

Hand the repurpose brief to the content agent.
It treats it like any approved brief.

## Repurpose Agent Constraints

- Never repurpose content that violated hard constraints
- Always check the repurpose log before proceeding
- Flag if original piece needs fact-checking before repurposing
