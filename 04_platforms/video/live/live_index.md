# live_index.md — Live Video Master Rules

## Purpose
Master rules for all live content.
Applies across YouTube, X, Twitch, Kick.
Same rules across all platforms.

---

## Live Philosophy

Live is a structured container for authentic
real-time execution. Not scripted content performed live.
The run of show is the spine. The ability to riff
is the muscle. The structure enables the freedom.

---

## Default Live Format

Default length: 1-2 hours.
Preferred ceiling: 2 hours.
Maximum: 4 hours only with enough content to justify.

Never go long because there's nothing to cut.
Go long because there's too much value to stop.

---

## Live Show Types

### Solo Show
Host alone. Direct to audience.
Best for: hot takes, framework breakdowns,
building-in-public updates, Q&A.

### Interview or Guest Show
Host plus one guest.
Best for: founder stories, expert frameworks.

### Panel Show
Host plus multiple guests.
Best for: debate, multi-perspective topics.

### Casual or Audience Show
Host engaging directly with viewers or playing/working
on something live.
Best for: relationship building, casual engagement.

---

## Universal Live Rules

- Cold open immediately. No "is this working?" before going live.
- State the show topic in the first 60 seconds.
- Acknowledge chat but don't be held hostage by it.
- Re-engagement beat every 15-20 minutes for late joiners.
- Hard stop at the planned end time.
- One CTA per show. Stated at 75% mark and again at close.

---

## Re-engagement for Late Joiners

Every 15-20 minutes, one of:
- "If you just joined — here's what we're doing."
- Restate the show topic and where we are.
- A question to chat that resets engagement.
