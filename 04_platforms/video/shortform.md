# shortform.md — Short Form Video

## Purpose
Rules for short form vertical video.
Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts — same rules for all.

---

## Hard Rules

- 15-60 seconds is the target. 90 seconds max.
- Hook must land in the first 2-3 seconds.
- No intro. No "hey guys welcome back."
- No outro longer than 5 seconds.
- Vertical 9:16 always.
- Captions always — 85% of short form watched without sound.
- Value per second is the only metric.

---

## The Two Hooks Rule

**Hook 1: The on-screen text hook**
Text that appears in the first 2-3 seconds.
Stops the scroll before audio kicks in.
3-8 words. Bold claim or specific result.

Example patterns:
"Why your [thing] will never [outcome]"
"0 to [number] in [timeframe]"
"The [topic] mistake [target role] make"

**Hook 2: The spoken hook**
The first words out of your mouth.
Must extend the on-screen hook — not repeat it.

If on-screen says "Why your community will never grow"
the spoken hook is: "Because you're optimizing for
reach when you should be optimizing for depth."

---

## Structure

**Seconds 0-3: The hook**
On-screen text plus spoken hook simultaneously.

**Seconds 3-45: The value**
One idea. Fully delivered.
No tangents. No sub-points.

**Seconds 45-60: The close**
Restate the core insight in one sentence.
One CTA — follow, comment, or link in bio.

---

## Brief Format for Short Form

[ON-SCREEN HOOK TEXT]
3-8 words. Bold claim.

[SPOKEN HOOK]
1-2 sentences. Escalates the on-screen hook.

[CORE VALUE — bullet points]
3-5 bullets max. Operator riffs from these.

[CLOSE PLUS CTA]
One sentence close. One CTA.

---

## Caption Rules

Caption is not a transcript. It's a second hook.
First line: restate or deepen the on-screen hook.
Never start caption with "In this video..."
Length: 1-3 sentences for Reels and Shorts.
Hashtags: 3-5 max. Relevant only. At the bottom.

---

## What Works in Short Form

- Single contrarian take with a proof point
- One-sentence lesson pulled from origin_story.md
- The most surprising stat or result with the mechanic
- A mistake most [TARGET ROLE] make with the fix
- A framework distilled to its single core insight
