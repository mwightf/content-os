# video_index.md — Video Master Rules

## Purpose
Master rules for all video content.
Routes input to the right format and style.
Every video file inherits from this.

---

## The One Rule Above All Others

Value per second is the only metric that matters.
Length is not a goal. Value density is.
Target length: 5-8 minutes for long form.
Hard ceiling: 10 minutes unless content earns every second.
Never pad to hit a length target.

---

## Format Router

### Step 1: What type of content is this?

Framework or system explanation: Whiteboard or talking head.
Personal story or building-in-public: Talking head or vlog.
Tutorial or how-to: Whiteboard or screen capture.
Hot take or single idea: Short form vertical.
Interview or conversation: Long form with interview format.
Live show: live/run_of_show.md

---

### Step 2: What style fits?

| Content type | Primary style | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Framework explanation | Whiteboard | Talking head |
| Personal story | Talking head | Vlog |
| Tutorial | Whiteboard or screen | Talking head |
| Hot take | Talking head | Short form |
| Interview | Two-shot | N/A |
| Building in public | Vlog | Talking head |
| Live show | Multi-cam or single cam | N/A |

---

### Step 3: Does this need a full script?

Full word-for-word script:
- Talking head content
- Storytelling and narrative content
- Anything going on teleprompter

Structured outline only:
- Whiteboard content (screen cues delivery)
- Tutorial content (steps cue delivery)
- Interview format (questions guide it)
- Live shows (run of show guides it)

---

## Universal Video Rules

- Hook in the first 3-5 seconds. No exceptions.
- No intro bumper before the hook. Ever.
- Channel intro comes AFTER the hook if at all.
- Every 60-90 seconds needs a re-engagement moment.
- End with one CTA only.
- No "smash that like button" as the primary CTA.

---

## Hook Types for Video

The result hook:
"In the next [TIME] I'm going to show you the exact
system that took [STARTING POINT] to [SPECIFIC RESULT]."

The provocation hook:
"Most [TARGET ROLE] are optimizing for the wrong
thing entirely."

The scene hook:
"It was [TIME]. We had [SPECIFIC SITUATION].
[TIMEFRAME] later, [WHAT CHANGED]."

The pattern interrupt hook:
"[UNEXPECTED CREDENTIAL OR EXPERIENCE] taught me
something about [TOPIC] that most people miss."

---

## Re-engagement Moments

Every 60-90 seconds, one of:
- A new question introduced
- A surprising data point or story beat
- A direct address to the viewer
- A visual change (whiteboard reveal, new slide)
- A stakes raise ("here's where most people get this wrong")

---

## CTA Rules for Video

One CTA per video. Placed in the final 30 seconds.

Framework video: "Full written breakdown in the newsletter."
Story video: "Follow to see how this continues to develop."
Tutorial video: "Try this today and reply to my latest post."
Building in public: "[PRODUCT or BRAND] is where this system lives."

---

## Description Rules

First 2 lines appear before "show more" — treat like a hook.
Rest of description: timestamps, links, CTA.
No keyword stuffing. Write for humans.
