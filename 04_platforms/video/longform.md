# longform.md — Long Form Video

## Purpose
Rules for long form video content.
YouTube primary. Applies to any recorded long form.
Covers all styles: talking head, whiteboard,
story/vlog, interview, and tutorial.

## The Prime Directive

Value per second above all else.
Target: 5-8 minutes.
Acceptable: 3-10 minutes.
Beyond 10 minutes: only if every second earns it.

---

## Style Router

### Talking Head
Best for: personal stories, hot takes, direct teaching.
Script: full word-for-word. Teleprompter ready.

### Whiteboard (digital or physical)
Best for: frameworks, systems, visual explanations.
Script: structured outline only.
The whiteboard cues delivery.

### Story or Vlog
Best for: building-in-public, origin story, behind-the-scenes.
Script: full script for narration sections.

### Tutorial or Screen Capture
Best for: how-to content, tool walkthroughs, system demos.
Script: outline only. Screen content cues delivery.

### Interview
Best for: guest conversations, founder stories.
Script: question list and run of show only.

## Universal Long Form Structure

**Hook (0:00-0:20):**
Most compelling thing about this video stated immediately.
No intro bumper first.

**Stakes (0:20-0:45):**
Why the viewer should watch to the end.
What they will know or be able to do by the end.

**Content body (0:45 to end minus 60 sec):**
The value. Re-engagement every 60-90 seconds.

**Close plus CTA (final 30-60 seconds):**
Restate the core insight. One CTA.

## Script Format: Talking Head and Story

Full word-for-word script. Teleprompter ready.

[HOOK]
Exact opening words. Must land under 20 seconds.

[STAKES]
Why watch to the end. One clear payoff.

[SECTION 1 HEADER]
Full script. Note b-roll or visual cues in [BRACKETS].

[RE-ENGAGEMENT MOMENT]
A question, stat, or stakes raise.

[SECTION 2 HEADER]
Full script continues.

[CLOSE]
Restate the core insight. One CTA.

---

## Script Format: Whiteboard

Structured outline. Not word-for-word.

[HOOK — spoken]
2-3 sentences max. Operator riffs from this.

[WHITEBOARD FLOW]
List of what gets written or drawn. In order.
Notes on what gets said at each beat.

[RE-ENGAGEMENT CHECKPOINTS]
Mark where re-engagement moments land.

[CLOSE PLUS CTA]

---

## Script Format: Interview

[PRE-SHOW BRIEF]
Guest name and background.
The one thing this conversation should leave viewers with.

[OPENING — spoken by host]
How the host introduces the guest.
Specific and earned — not a resume read.

[QUESTION SET]
Opening question (easy, builds rapport):
Core questions (3-5, the meat):
Curve ball question (unexpected angle):
Close question (legacy or advice):

[CLOSE]
Host's closing statement. CTA for guest and viewer.

## Title Rules

See 05_frameworks/video/youtube_titles.md for full guidance.

What works (example patterns):
"How I Built [SPECIFIC RESULT] With [SPECIFIC CONSTRAINT]"
"Why Your [THING] Will Never [OUTCOME]"
"A [UNEXPECTED CREDENTIAL]'s Guide to [TOPIC]"

What doesn't work:
"My thoughts on [topic]"
"THIS CHANGED EVERYTHING"

## Edit Notes Format

Include edit notes in [BRACKETS] throughout scripts.

[B-ROLL: description of what should be shown]
[GRAPHIC: description of on-screen graphic]
[WHITEBOARD: what gets written or drawn here]
[CUTAWAY: cut to this for visual variety]
[CHAPTER MARKER: title of chapter for YouTube]
