# linkedin_article.md — LinkedIn Article

## Hard Constraints (X and LinkedIn only)

- Sentences range from 150-200 characters.
- Hard line break after every sentence unless inside a list.
- No emojis.
- No em dashes, long dashes, or double hyphens used as dashes.
- No corporate language, filler phrases, or throat-clearing.
- Never start with "I" as the first word.
- Write like a person, not a brand account.

---

## When to Use LinkedIn Article vs Post

Use article when:
- The framework needs headers and sections
- The piece is a reference someone would bookmark
- The argument takes more than 1300 characters
- You want it to rank for a specific topic
- It's a flagship piece (origin story, major framework)

Use post when:
- Single insight or story
- No need for headers or sections
- Under 1300 characters

---

## Structure

**Title (6-10 words):**
Specific over clever.

Patterns that work:
"How I Built [SPECIFIC RESULT] With [SPECIFIC CONSTRAINT]"
"The [TOPIC] System Every [TARGET ROLE] Needs"
"What [UNEXPECTED EXPERIENCE] Taught Me About [TOPIC]"

**Opening section (no header):**
First 2-3 sentences are the hook.
Must make the reader feel the problem before the solution.

**Body sections (with H2 headers):**
- 3-5 sections max
- Each section equals one idea fully developed
- Header is a statement, not a question
- 150-300 words per section
- One concrete example per section minimum

**Closing section:**
- Summary of the core argument
- The transformation — where the reader is now vs before
- Single CTA

---

## Length
- Minimum: 800 words
- Sweet spot: 1000-1500 words
- Maximum: 2000 words
