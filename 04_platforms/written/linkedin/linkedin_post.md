# linkedin_post.md — LinkedIn Post

## Hard Constraints (X and LinkedIn only)

- Sentences range from 150-200 characters.
- Hard line break after every sentence unless inside a list.
- No emojis.
- No em dashes, long dashes, or double hyphens used as dashes.
- No corporate language, filler phrases, or throat-clearing.
- Never start with "I" as the first word.
- Write like a person, not a brand account.

---

## Hard Rules

- First 2-3 lines appear before "see more" cutoff
- Never start with "I am excited to announce"
- Never start with "I am humbled"
- Never start with "Grateful for..."
- No more than 3 lines before a line break
- Max 1300 characters for standard posts
- No more than 3-5 hashtags, at the bottom only
- One CTA at the end, never in the middle
- Links in comments, not in post body

---

## The LinkedIn Preview Rule

LinkedIn shows the first 2-3 lines before "see more."
Those lines are the entire game.
Apply all hook rules from x_hooks.md to lines 1-3.

---

## Structure

**Opening (2-3 lines — the hook):**
Bold claim, specific result, or pattern interrupt.
Short sentences. White space after line 3.

**Body:**
- Short paragraphs, 1-3 lines each
- White space between every paragraph
- One idea per paragraph
- Concrete example or number every 3-4 paragraphs

**Close:**
- Reframe or key takeaway
- One question or reflection to drive comments
- Or one direct CTA

---

## Length Targets

| Type | Target |
|---|---|
| Short punchy post | 300-500 chars |
| Standard post | 600-900 chars |
| Long post | 1000-1300 chars |

## Opening Hook Patterns

"Most [TARGET ROLE] I talk to have the same problem.

[2-3 lines naming the problem in their words.]

It's not a [perceived] problem. It's a [reframed] problem."

---

"[Specific result or specific failure from the fact bank].

Here's what I know now that I didn't know then —
and what I'd tell every [TARGET ROLE] before they
[avoid this mistake]."
