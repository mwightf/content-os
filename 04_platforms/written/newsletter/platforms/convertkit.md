## Philosophy
ConvertKit audiences skew toward plain text.
Plain text emails feel more personal.
Plain text emails have better deliverability.

## Formatting
- Plain text or minimal HTML only
- No heavy headers or section dividers
- One or two line breaks between paragraphs

## Welcome Sequence Outline (template)
Email 1 (immediate): Who the operator is + the core promise
Email 2 (day 2): The brand's contrarian belief from values.md
Email 3 (day 4): The origin story (from origin_story.md)
Email 4 (day 7): The flagship framework intro
Email 5 (day 10): The product or offer — what it is and why it exists
Email 6 (day 14): Best content from the archive
Email 7 (day 21): The ask — join the community or follow
