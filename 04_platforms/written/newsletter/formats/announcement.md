# announcement.md — Newsletter Announcement Format

## Purpose
For product news, launches, updates, and milestones.
The news IS the issue.

---

## When to Use

- Product or feature launches
- Event announcements
- New content series or format launches
- Milestones worth sharing with the audience

---

## Rules

- Lead with the news immediately. No buildup.
- State why it matters to the reader in sentence 2.
- Never make it about how excited YOU are.
- One announcement per issue.

---

## Structure

**Subject line:**
Names the news directly.
"[PRODUCT NAME] Now Supports [FEATURE]."
"[EVENT NAME] is Back. Here's What's Happening."

**Opening (2-3 lines):**
State the news.
State why it matters to the reader.
No "I'm so excited to share."

**The what (1-2 paragraphs):**
What exactly is being announced.
Specific features, dates, or details.

**The why it matters (1-2 paragraphs):**
Why the reader should care.
Frame it from their perspective.

**The proof or context (1 paragraph):**
Why this is credible.

**The ask (CTA):**
One clear action. Link on its own line.

---

## Length
300-500 words.
