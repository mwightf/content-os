# story.md — Newsletter Story Format

## Purpose
For issues where the insight comes through a personal
narrative or experience. The story IS the content.

---

## When to Use

- Building-in-public moments
- Lessons from a specific event or decision
- Origin story content (pull from origin_story.md)
- Story assets from the brand fact bank
- Failures and what they cost

---

## Structure

**Subject line:**
Personal and specific. Hints at the story.
"I Lost [SPECIFIC STAKE]. Here's What I Learned."
"[SPECIFIC SCENE] That Changed Everything."

**Opening hook (3-5 lines):**
Drop into the scene. Time. Place. Stakes.
No setup. No context. Into the moment.

"It was [YEAR].
We had [SPECIFIC SITUATION].
[TIMEFRAME] later, [WHAT CHANGED]."

**The before (1-2 paragraphs):**
Where things stood before the moment.
Keep it tight — readers are here for the turn.

**The turn (1-2 paragraphs):**
The moment everything changed.
Be specific. Be honest. Don't soften it.

**The aftermath (1-2 paragraphs):**
What happened as a result. Real consequences.
Real numbers if available.

**The lesson extracted (1-2 paragraphs):**
What the story teaches. State it plainly.
One lesson only — the sharpest one.

**Close plus CTA:**
One line reflection. One CTA.

---

## Tone
Most personal format in the system.
Vulnerability is allowed here — earn it with specifics.
No manufactured emotion. Just tell what happened.

---

## Length
600-900 words.
