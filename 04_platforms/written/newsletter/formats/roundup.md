# roundup.md — Newsletter Roundup Format

## Purpose
For issues that cover multiple items, links, or topics.
Modular structure. Each item is self-contained.

---

## When to Use

- Weekly digest of what's happening in the space
- Resources worth reading or watching
- Multiple small lessons from one week
- Curated picks for the ICP

---

## Structure

**Subject line:**
Specific to the most valuable item.
Not "This week's roundup."
"The Algorithm Change Founders Need to Know About."

**Opening hook (2-3 lines):**
Frame the week or theme connecting the items.
No "here's what I've been up to this week."

**Item structure (repeat 3-5 times):**

[ITEM HEADER — bold, 3-6 words]

One paragraph explaining the item.
Why it matters to the reader.
What to do with it.

[Link or resource if applicable]

**Close plus CTA:**
One line that ties the items together.
One CTA.

---

## Rules

- 3-5 items max.
- Each item: 3-6 sentences only.
- Every item must be relevant to the ICP.
- No filler items to hit a number.
- If you only have 2 strong items — use essay.md.
- Lead with the strongest item, not the most recent.

---

## Length
400-700 words total.
