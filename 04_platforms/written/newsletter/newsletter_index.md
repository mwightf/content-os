# newsletter_index.md — Newsletter Master Rules

## Purpose
Master rules for all newsletter content.
Every newsletter format file inherits from this.
When format files conflict with this file — this wins.

---

## Hard Constraints

- One CTA per issue. No exceptions.
- One topic per issue. No exceptions.
- Subject line: 6-8 words max.
- Preview text: extends the subject line, never repeats it.
- No "hey friends" or warm-up preamble. Ever.
- Links go at the end of sections, never mid-paragraph.

---

## Subject Line Rules

Formats that work:
- The specific result: "[Starting point] to [End point] in [time]"
- The provocative truth: "Your [thing] is already [bad outcome]"
- The direct promise: "The [topic] system, full stack"
- The curiosity gap: "What [unexpected reference] never had to build"
- The personal story: "I lost [specific stake]. Here's what I learned."

Rules:
- 6-8 words max
- Sentence case always
- No clickbait that the issue doesn't deliver on
- No exclamation marks
- No ALL CAPS

---

## Preview Text Rules

- Never repeat the subject line
- Extend the tension or promise
- 80-100 characters max

Examples:
Subject: "Your community is already dead"
Preview: "Most founders don't find out until it's too late."

Subject: "I lost [specific stake]. Here's what I learned."
Preview: "The [mistake category] mistake that cost the most."

---

## Opening Hook Rules

First 2-3 lines before any content or context.
No warm-up. No "this week I've been thinking about."
Drop the reader into the thing immediately.

Wrong:
"Hey everyone, hope you're having a great week.
This week I want to talk about something that's
been on my mind."

Right:
"Most newsletters die in the first 90 days.
Not because the writer ran out of ideas.
Because they never built a reason for people to stay."

---

## Body Rules

- Short paragraphs: 2-4 sentences max
- One idea per paragraph
- Hard line break between every paragraph
- Concrete example or proof point every 3-4 paragraphs
- Headers for issues over 600 words

---

## CTA Rules

One ask. At the end. Always.

- Growing subscribers: "Forward this to one [TARGET ROLE] who needs it."
- Driving to product: "[PRODUCT NAME] is built for this. Link below."
- Driving to YouTube: "Full video breakdown is live. Link below."
- Driving replies: "Reply and tell me your biggest challenge. I read every one."

---

## Closing Rules

- Sign off as the operator, not as a brand
- One line max: "Until next time, [FIRST NAME]"
- No "best regards" or formal sign-offs

---

## Format Selection Guide

| Input type | Use this format |
|---|---|
| Personal story with lesson | story.md |
| Multiple items or links | roundup.md |
| Single argument or framework | essay.md |
| Product news or launch | announcement.md |

When in doubt — use essay.md.
