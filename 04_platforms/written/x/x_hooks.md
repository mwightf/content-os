# x_hooks.md — Hook Library for X

## Purpose
A living library of proven hook formats for X.
Indexed by type. Updated as hooks are tested.
Last updated: [DATE]

Hooks are platform-specific. What works on X is
different from what works on LinkedIn or in a newsletter.

---

## The X Hook Rules

- First line only — hooks live and die in one sentence
- Never start with "I"
- Never start with a yes/no question
- Must create tension, curiosity, or pattern interrupt
- Specific always beats general
- Numbers beat adjectives every time
- The hook must be true — no bait and switch

---

## Category 1: The Specific Result

Templates:
"[Number] [result] in [timeframe]."
"[X] to [Y] in [timeframe]. Here's the mechanic."
"[Did hard thing]. Here's what it actually cost."

Fill these with numbers from the fact bank in brand_index.md.

---

## Category 2: The Provocative Truth

Templates:
"[Common thing] is not [what people think it is]."
"Most [people] are wrong about [topic]. Here's why."
"[Counterintuitive truth]. Let me explain."

---

## Category 3: The Pattern Interrupt

Templates:
"[Unexpected context] taught me [unexpected lesson]."
"[Surprising credential] turned into [unexpected outcome]."

Pull from story assets in origin_story.md — the
specific moments that nobody else can claim.

---

## Category 4: The Direct Question

Rules:
- Never a yes/no question
- Must be a question they feel personally
- Must be answerable by the content

Templates:
"Why do some [things] [outcome] and most just [fail mode]?"
"What's the difference between [common thing]
and [thing that wins]?"
"If [scenario] tomorrow — would [test of the brand]?"

---

## Category 5: The Contrast Hook

Templates:
"Most [people] do [X]. The ones that win do [Y]."
"[Group A watches]. [Group B moves].
Here's how to build [the second one]."

---

## Category 6: The Empathy Hook

Templates:
"You're doing [right things] and it's still not working."
"You built [something real]. Nobody knows about it.
That's a [reframed] problem, not a [perceived] problem."
