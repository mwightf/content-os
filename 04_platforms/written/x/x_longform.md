# x_longform.md — X Long Form

## Hard Constraints (X and LinkedIn only)

- Sentences range from 150-200 characters.
- Hard line break after every sentence unless inside a list.
- No emojis.
- No em dashes, long dashes, or double hyphens used as dashes.
- No corporate language, filler phrases, or throat-clearing.
- Never start with "I" as the first word.
- Write like a person, not a brand account.

---

## When to Use X Long Form vs Thread

Use X long form when:
- The piece is a continuous argument
- The reader needs to read it as a whole
- It's more essay than framework

Use a thread when:
- Each point can stand alone
- The content is step-by-step or list-based

---

## Structure

**Opening (first 2-3 lines):**
Same rules as x_post.md hook.
Must hook before the "read more" cutoff.
These lines appear in the feed — they are the ad
for the rest of the piece.

**Body:**
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- White space between every paragraph
- One idea per paragraph
- Concrete examples every 3-4 paragraphs

**Close:**
- Reframe or summary of the core argument
- One CTA
- Should feel like a landing, not a stop

---

## Length
- Minimum: 400 words
- Sweet spot: 600-900 words
- Maximum: 1200 words

---

## Topics Best Suited for X Long Form

- Origin story pieces
- Deep framework explainers
- Building-in-public retrospectives
- Contrarian arguments that need full development
- Lessons from a major win or loss
