# Video Hook Formats — Hormozi Style
Source: Alex Hormozi hook patterns.
Use as structural reference — never copy verbatim.

---

## Purpose

These are script hooks — the opening lines of a video.
Not titles. Not captions.
The first words out of the operator's mouth on camera.

Use when:
- Topic is results, performance, business, or transformation
- Direct and slightly confrontational tone fits
- Content is about fixing, improving, or revealing

Do not use when:
- The video is primarily warm story or connection content

---

## How to Use These

Do not copy verbatim.

---

## Group A: Calling Out Mistakes

Use when the video corrects a wrong belief or action.

Structures:
"Most people are doing [X] wrong. Here's how to do it right."
"The one thing holding you back from [outcome]."
"Why [common advice] is wrong."

---

## Group B: Personal Losses and Lessons

Use when the video is built around a story
from the brand fact bank.

Structures:
"I lost [specific amount] because of this. Don't repeat it."
"If I had to start over, I would do this."
"The [number] lessons I learned the hard way."

---

## Group C: Direct Promises and Results

Use when the video delivers a specific measurable outcome.

Structures:
"Here's how I [result] in [timeframe]."
"If you want [result], start doing this."

---

## Group D: Harsh Truths and Urgency

Use sparingly. Only when content genuinely delivers on it.

Structures:
"Here's the harsh truth about [topic]."
"The real reason you're not seeing results."

---

## Group E: Secrets and Contrarian Takes

Use when the video challenges conventional wisdom.

Structures:
"Why [common advice] is completely wrong."
"Here's what nobody tells you about [topic]."

---

## Group F: Start From Zero

Use for aspirational content targeted at early-stage ICP.

Structures:
"If I had to build [WHAT] from zero today
knowing what I know now, here's the exact
sequence I would follow in the first [TIMEFRAME]."

---

## Group G: Identity and Separation

Use when the video is about what top performers do differently.

Structures:
"The [TARGET ROLE] who [WIN MODE] instead of [LOSE MODE]
all have one thing in common.
It's not the [obvious answer]. Here's what it actually is."

---

## Group H: Tested and Proven

Use when the operator has run an experiment or done research.

Structures:
"We ran [NUMBER] [WHAT].
Here's the one variable that predicted success
every single time — and it's not what we expected."
