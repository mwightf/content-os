# The Hero's Journey — Joseph Campbell

## Source
Joseph Campbell — "The Hero with a Thousand Faces" (1949).
In branded content: the operator plays the mentor figure (step 4).
The ICP plays the hero. The content is the elixir
the hero is given to take back to their world.

---

## The 11 Stages (condensed for content use)

1. ORDINARY WORLD — life before the call
2. CALL TO ADVENTURE — the disruption or opportunity
3. REFUSAL OF THE CALL — the hesitation and doubt
4. MEETING THE MENTOR — the guide who gives tools
5. CROSSING THE THRESHOLD — committing to the journey
6. TESTS, ALLIES, AND ENEMIES — the hard middle
7. THE ORDEAL — the darkest moment
8. THE REWARD — the insight or truth earned
9. THE ROAD BACK — applying the lesson to the real world
10. RESURRECTION — the final test before home
11. RETURN WITH THE ELIXIR — back home, sharing what was earned

## How to Use in Content

Position the ICP as the hero.
The operator is the mentor — step 4.
Content meets them where they are in the journey.
Most ICPs sit between steps 2 and 6.
They need a mentor. That's the role the content plays.

Use the ordeal for credibility.
Content that skips to the elixir feels hollow.
The ordeal is what earns the right to teach.

Use the elixir as the CTA.
"Here's what I'm giving you that I had to earn the hard way."
