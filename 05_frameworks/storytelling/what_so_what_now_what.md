# What / So What / Now What

## Purpose
The clearest framework for educational content.
Forces three things most content skips:
stating the insight, explaining why it matters,
and telling the reader what to do with it.

---

## The Three Beats

WHAT:
State the insight or fact clearly.
One sentence. No setup.

SO WHAT:
Explain why it matters to this specific reader.
Connect the insight to their situation, goal, or frustration.

NOW WHAT:
Tell them what to do with it.
One concrete action or reframe.
Leave them with something they can use today.

---

## When to Use

Best fit:
- Educational posts on X and LinkedIn
- Newsletter body sections
- Framework explainer content
- LinkedIn articles (each section follows this pattern)

---

## Template

Example 1: Generic structure

WHAT: "[One-sentence insight from a content pillar.]"

SO WHAT: "[2-3 sentences. Why this matters to the ICP
specifically. The cost of ignoring it.
Specific consequence stated plainly.]"

NOW WHAT: "[This week — one concrete action the reader
can take. Specific enough to start today.
Small enough to actually do.]"

---

Example 2: Distribution insight

WHAT: "Distribution is not the same as posting."

SO WHAT: "Posting is putting content into the world.
Distribution is making sure the right person
sees it at the right moment.
Most operators post. Almost none distribute."

NOW WHAT: "For every piece of content you create —
identify one specific person or community
where it should land beyond your own feed.
That is distribution."

---

## Combining With Other Frameworks

Newsletter essay structure using multiple frameworks:
- Opening: PAS or Contrarian (creates tension)
- Body section 1: What/So What/Now What (delivers insight 1)
- Body section 2: What/So What/Now What (delivers insight 2)
- Close: BAB (shows the transformation)
