# Dan Harmon's Story Circle

## Source
Dan Harmon — creator of Community and Rick and Morty.
Simplified version of Campbell's Hero's Journey.
8 steps. Character ends where they began
but fundamentally changed by the journey.

---

## The 8 Steps

1. YOU — the character in their ordinary world
2. NEED — something is missing or wrong
3. GO — crosses a threshold into the unknown
4. SEARCH — looks for what they need, adapts, tries
5. FIND — gets what they were looking for
6. TAKE — but it comes at a cost
7. RETURN — comes back to where they started
8. CHANGE — but they are not the same

---

## How to Use the Story Circle

For video scripts: the viewer is YOU. Their struggle is NEED.
Content takes them through the journey.

For long-form newsletters: open in the YOU state.
End in the CHANGE state.

For building-in-public content: the operator IS the character.
Real stories mapped to the circle create the most resonant content.

Source stories from 01_brand/origin_story.md.
The story assets there can each be mapped to the
8 beats for fully developed long-form pieces.
