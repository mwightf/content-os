# The Contrarian Take Framework

## Purpose
For content that challenges conventional wisdom.
Creates instant tension. Tension creates engagement.

---

## The Four Beats

CONVENTIONAL WISDOM:
State what everyone believes.
Be fair to it — don't strawman.
The reader should recognize this as something they've heard.

THE FLIP:
State the opposite.
The thing you actually believe to be true.
Make it clean and direct — one sentence if possible.
Pull from the contrarian belief in 01_brand/values.md.

THE PROOF:
Show why the flip is right.
Evidence. Real examples. Real numbers.
Pull from the fact bank in 01_brand/brand_index.md.
Without proof it's just noise. With proof it's a reframe.

THE IMPLICATION:
What does this mean for the reader?
Leave them with a changed behavior, not just a changed belief.

---

## When to Use

Best fit:
- X hook posts
- LinkedIn opening posts
- Newsletter openers
- YouTube video premises and titles

---

## Template

Example 1: Generic structure

CW: "[Common belief in your space]."
Flip: "[The opposite — what the brand actually believes]."
Proof: "[Specific number, story, or case from the fact bank
that proves the flip is right.]"
Implication: "[What the reader should do or stop doing
based on the reframe.]"

---

## The Competitor Test

After writing a contrarian take, ask:
Could my biggest competitor post this exact take?
If yes — you haven't gone specific enough.
Add real numbers, real stories, or real experience
from the fact bank.
The flip is generic. The proof is proprietary.
