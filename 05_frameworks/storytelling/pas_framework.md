# Problem-Agitation-Solution (PAS)

## Source
Classic direct response copywriting framework.
Meets the reader in their pain before offering any solution.

---

## The Three Beats

PROBLEM:
Name the exact pain the reader has right now.
Be precise enough that the reader feels seen.

Wrong: "Many [target role] struggle with [generic topic]."
Right: "Most [target role] are [doing the specific thing]
in [specific failure mode]."

AGITATION:
Make them feel why this problem is actually costing them.
Show the consequences of the problem going unsolved.
This is where most content gets soft — don't.

Wrong: "This can be challenging for your business."
Right: "So they [tactic 1]. [Specific failure mode].
They [tactic 2]. [Another failure mode].
They watch [worse competitor] beat them because
[competitor] has [the asset they don't]
and they have [the rented version]."

SOLUTION:
Name the solution clearly and let the body deliver it.
Do not over-explain here.

Wrong: "That's why I've developed a comprehensive approach."
Right: "The fix isn't [more of the same].
It's [the specific reframe or system].
Here's how it works."

---

## When to Use PAS

Best fit:
- X posts and threads
- LinkedIn posts
- Newsletter openings
- Short-form video hooks

Not the best fit:
- Long-form video scripts (use Story Circle)
- Origin story content (use Hero's Journey)

---

## Short Form PAS Template (X post version)

"Most [TARGET ROLE] [have specific situation]
and [missing piece].

So they [do common tactic 1].
[Do common tactic 2].
And still watch [worse outcome].

The problem isn't [perceived cause].
It's that [reframed cause]."
