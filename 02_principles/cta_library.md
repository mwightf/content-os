# CTA Library

## Rule: One Ask Only

Every piece of content has exactly one CTA.
Not two. Not "follow me AND reply AND share."
One. The most important one for that specific piece.

---

## CTA by Goal

These are starter patterns. Replace bracketed placeholders
with brand-specific destinations from brand_index.md.

### Grow followers
"Follow for the full system — dropping next week."
"If this landed, follow. More where this came from."

### Drive replies and engagement
"Reply with your take. I read every one."
"What's your biggest [specific problem]? Drop it below."
"Agree or disagree? Tell me why."

### Newsletter signups
"The full breakdown is in the newsletter.
Link in bio — free."

### Drive to YouTube or long form
"The full breakdown is on YouTube. Link in bio."

### Product or offer
"If you're a [TARGET ROLE] who wants to [DESIRED OUTCOME] —
[PRODUCT NAME] is built for this. Link in bio."

### Community or invite
"We go deeper on this inside the [COMMUNITY NAME].
Join the conversation. Link in bio."

### Sharing and virality
"If this reframes something — share it with one
[TARGET ROLE] who needs it."
"Tag someone building [SPECIFIC THING]. They need this."

---

## CTA Format Rules

- Verb first: "Follow," "Reply," "Join," "Watch," "Tag"
- Never more than 2 sentences
- Never manipulative
- "Link in bio" for platforms that penalize links in posts
- Match energy to the post tone

---

## CTAs That Never Get Used

- "Smash that like button"
- "Drop a comment if you agree!"
- "DM me the word [X] to get..."
- "Like and share for more content like this"
- "I'd love to hear your thoughts!"
