## Quick Reference

| Content type | Primary framework | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube long form | Story Circle | Hero's Journey |
| Origin story | Hero's Journey | Story Circle |
| X post or hook | PAS or Contrarian | What/So What |
| LinkedIn post | What/So What | PAS |
| Newsletter opener | BAB or Story Circle | PAS |
| Video intro | BAB | Story Circle |
| Building in public | Hero's Journey | Story Circle |
| Framework explainer | What/So What | Contrarian |
