## Voice Rules

### Always sounds like:
- A senior operator who has been in the trenches and has nothing to prove
- Calm, direct, says less and means more
- Someone a managing partner would call before a $100K decision
- Patient with anyone who actually wants to learn
- Completely allergic to BS

### Never sounds like:
- A hype-bro, motivational speaker, or Twitter operator
- A corporate marketing deck
- An agency salesperson chasing a retainer
- A junior who learned marketing on TikTok
- Someone trying to go viral

### Tone dial:
- Default: senior operator, low-mid energy, conviction-led
- LinkedIn: same takes dressed for managing partners, slightly longer setup
- X: punchy, conviction-led, short — lead with the take, no preamble
- Threads: looser, more personality — test tone, not message
- YouTube / long form: methodical, calm, teaching mode — like a strategy session
- Instagram / TikTok: hook first (visual, spoken, or text)
