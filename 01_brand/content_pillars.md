# Content Pillars — Matt Khorsandi (GLP Marketing)

## How to Use This File

Every piece of content lives in at least one pillar.
Content that doesn't fit a pillar doesn't get made.

---

## Pillar 1: SEO for Law Firms

**Core belief:**
Organic search is the only growth channel that compounds.
Every month you skip it, your competitors take more ground.

**Topics:**
- Local SEO for legal
- On-page strategy for practice areas
- Content systems and editorial cadence
- Link acquisition for law firms
- Technical SEO audits
- Keyword strategy by practice area
- Scorpion / closed-CMS migrations
- Geo and manufacturer page systems (lemon law, PI, family)

**Signature angle:**
Treat SEO like a P&L line item, not a marketing tactic.
Cases signed per keyword — not rankings.

---

## Pillar 2: AI Search Visibility (GEO)

**Core belief:**
Google's monopoly on legal search is breaking. Firms that
get cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews
in 2026 will own the next decade.

**Topics:**
- How AI engines pick sources
- What makes a firm "citable" to an LLM
- Structured data and entity SEO
- Brand building for LLMs
- The death of the 10 blue links
- Practical playbooks for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude,
  Gemini, Copilot, Grok, and Google AI Overviews
- Live client examples (Onyx in AI Overviews, etc.)

**Signature angle:**
Building the playbook for AI visibility in legal in real
time, with named clients and tracked results — not theory,
not acronym salad.

---

## Pillar 3: Fractional CMO Thinking for Legal

**Core belief:**
Most law firms don't have a marketing problem. They have a
leadership problem. Without a CMO-level brain, marketing
money gets wasted.

**Topics:**
- Positioning and offer design for firms
- Pricing and packaging
- Client acquisition systems
- Retention and referral programs
- Marketing org design and hiring
- How to read a marketing P&L
- When to insource vs. outsource

**Signature angle:**
Think like a partner, not a vendor. Show up to QBRs talking
about EBITDA, not impressions.

---

## Pillar 4: Accountability Over Vanity Metrics

**Core belief:**
If your marketing report doesn't show cases signed and
revenue, your agency is hiding from accountability.

**Topics:**
- The only KPIs that matter
- How to fire your agency
- What real reporting looks like
- How to audit a marketing spend
- ROI math for legal marketing
- The "QBR test" — what a real review looks like

**Signature angle:**
Publish actual client numbers and lose deals over honest
reporting. Most agencies couldn't survive a real QBR.

---

## Pillar 5: Client Conversations (Case Studies)

**Core belief:**
Case studies should sound like a partner explaining how
they grew — not a sales deck.

**Topics:**
- Long-form interviews with GLP clients
- Behind-the-scenes problem solving
- Real numbers, real friction, real outcomes
- Migration stories (Scorpion → WordPress, etc.)
- AI visibility client breakdowns

**Signature angle:**
Sit across from the partners we serve and let them tell
the story. No b-roll, no script. Just operators talking shop.

---

## Pillar 6: Operator Thinking / Building GLP in Public

**Core belief:**
People don't follow agencies. They follow operators.
Showing the work builds the brand.

**Topics:**
- How GLP runs day to day
- Hiring decisions and team design
- Systems and SOPs
- Decisions made and unmade
- Mistakes and lessons
- The build toward the Hormozi/Martell tier

**Signature angle:**
Not pretending to have it figured out. Showing the build —
including the parts that aren't pretty.

---

## Pillar Hierarchy

**Primary (carries 80% of the content load):**
Pillars 1, 2, 3, 5 — SEO, AI visibility, fractional CMO
thinking, client conversations.

**Secondary (expand audience and build personal brand):**
Pillars 4 and 6 — accountability over vanity, and operator
thinking / building GLP in public.

**By ICP:**
Primary ICP (managing partners $5M-$50M): Pillars 1, 2, 3, 5
Secondary ICP (rising partners, marketing leads): Pillars 1, 2, 4
Tertiary ICP (other operators, fractional CMOs): Pillars 3, 4, 6
