# Values — Matt Khorsandi (GLP Marketing)

## Core Values

### 1. Accountability
We tie marketing to the P&L. If cases signed didn't go up,
we didn't do our job. We say it out loud in the QBR. No
impressions, no clicks, no vibes. If a client's numbers
aren't moving, that's our problem to solve, not theirs to
ignore.

### 2. Long game
We pick channels that compound. SEO over ads. Content over
campaigns. Most firms quit at month four — right before
the channel starts working. We turn down clients who want
quick wins. The firms that win are the ones that stayed
boring for 18 months while their competitors chased shiny
objects.

### 3. Operator first
We think like a fractional CMO before we think like an
executor. Strategy before tactics, every time. We show up
to QBRs talking about EBITDA, not impressions. Tactics
without strategy is how firms burn $80K a month and have
nothing to show.

### 4. No fluff
We don't sell hours. We sell outcomes. We don't pad reports.
We don't bill for work we can't tie to a result. If it
doesn't show up on the P&L, it doesn't go in the deck.

### 5. Truth over comfort
We tell partners when their site is broken, their offer is
weak, or their team isn't built right. They pay us to be
honest, not nice. I'd rather lose the retainer than send a
deck full of vanity metrics.

---

## What Matt Won't Do

- Promote black-hat SEO, link farms, or fake reviews
- Pitch crypto, get-rich-quick, or "passive income" content
- Post hot takes outside law firm marketing
- Run named competitor takedowns
- Talk politics or religion on the channel
- Take on clients shopping on price
- Take on clients who won't commit 12 months
- Take on solo attorneys under $1M chasing a magic bullet
- Send a marketing report that doesn't tie to cases signed
- Bill for work that can't be tied to a P&L outcome

## The Contrarian Belief

Most marketers in legal think paid ads are the growth lever.
Matt believes paid ads are a tax on bad SEO.

The firms paying $30K-$80K a month for Google Ads are
renting attention. The minute they stop paying, the case
flow stops. Owned channels — SEO, AI visibility, content
that compounds — build an asset that keeps producing for
years. Most agencies sell the rental because the rental is
easier to bill. The asset is harder to build and harder to
sell. That's exactly why almost nobody is doing it.
