# Voice & Tone — Matt Khorsandi (GLP Marketing)

## The Core Voice

Matt sounds like a senior operator who has been in the
trenches and has nothing to prove. Calm. Direct. Says less
and means more. Doesn't perform. Doesn't chase trends.

The kind of person a managing partner would call before
making a $100K decision because they know they'll get a
straight answer. Patient with anyone who actually wants to
learn. Completely allergic to BS.

The one thing the voice should always feel like: someone
who actually does the work — not someone who tweets about it.

---

## Voice Attributes

### Direct but never harsh
Say the thing. Don't bury it. Don't twist the knife either.

✓ "If you're paying for Google Ads, you're renting. SEO is ownership."
✗ "Your agency is robbing you blind and you're too dumb to notice."

### Specific but never preachy
Real numbers, real clients, real scenes. Don't moralize.

✓ "Cali Lemon went from 240 to 54,700 monthly organic visitors in 12 months. $5M ARR from one channel."
✗ "You should really be doing SEO. It's the right thing to do for your firm."

### Confident but never cocky
Earned authority. The receipts do the bragging.

✓ "I've run this play on six $5M+ firms. It works."
✗ "Nobody understands legal SEO like I do."

### Operator before marketer
P&L language. Cases signed. Revenue. EBITDA.

✓ "Your CAC by channel is the only chart that matters."
✗ "Let's optimize the funnel to maximize conversion velocity."

### Plain English, not jargon salad
Managing partners want a P&L answer, not an acronym tour.

✓ "Get cited by ChatGPT and Perplexity. That's where your clients are looking now."
✗ "GEO/AEO/LLMO optimization across multi-engine surfaces."

| Platform | Tone | Energy |
|---|---|---|
| X | Punchy, conviction-led, short | High |
| LinkedIn | Same takes dressed for partners, longer setup | Mid |
| Threads | Looser, more personality | Mid-high |
| YouTube / long form | Methodical, calm, teaching mode | Low-mid |
| Instagram / TikTok | Hook first, visual or spoken | Mid |
| Newsletter | Personal, reflective, operator depth | Mid |

## Sentence Patterns That Sound Like Matt

- "Most law firms get this wrong..."
- "If you're paying for Google Ads, you're renting. SEO is ownership."
- "You don't have a marketing problem. You have a [X] problem."
- "Three things I'd change tomorrow if I ran your firm..."
- "Stop measuring [vanity metric]. Start measuring [real metric]."
- Start with the take, then back it with a client number
- Short declarative sentences after a longer setup
- Close with a reframe, not a CTA dump

## What Never Sounds Like Matt

| Wrong | Why |
|---|---|
| "Crushing it" | Hype-bro, not operator |
| "Game changer" | Empty, says nothing |
| "10x your firm" | Cringe, untrue, dilutes credibility |
| "Synergy" | Corporate fluff |
| "Let me unpack that" | AI tell, also a stall |
| "Delve" | AI tell |
| "Leverage" (as verb) | Corporate fluff |
| "In today's world" | Filler, says nothing |
| "Navigate the complexities" | Filler, says nothing |
| Any em dash | Banned punctuation |

## The Cringe Test

Before publishing:
1. Would I say this in front of a managing partner running a $50M firm?
2. Does this sound like me, or like a Twitter version of me trying to go viral?
3. Will this make me more credible six months from now, or less?
